Support

Invisiko is a staff management app for restaurants and takeaways - attendance, payroll, food safety compliance, rota and stock, in one app.

Common questions

I'm a staff member and don't have an account yet

Ask your manager for your restaurant's invite code, then use "Join a team" in the app to create your account with it.

Check-in says I'm not at the venue

Check-in confirms your phone's GPS position is within range of the venue. Make sure Location access is allowed for the app, and that you're actually on-site - a weak GPS signal indoors can occasionally read a few metres off.

I forgot my password

Use "Forgot password" on the login screen - a reset link is emailed to the address on your account.

Something isn't working, or I found a bug

Email us (below) with what you were doing and what happened, and we'll look into it.
