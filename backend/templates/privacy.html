Privacy Policy

Invisiko · Last updated 8 October 2026

Invisiko ("the app") is a staff management app used by a restaurant or takeaway ("the business") to run attendance, payroll, food safety compliance and stock management for its own employees. This policy explains what personal data the app collects, why, and how it is handled.

The business you work for (or that employs your staff, if you are a manager) controls the account and decides who is invited to use it. Invisiko provides the app and the infrastructure it runs on.

What we collect

  • Account details - name, email address, phone number, and your role (staff or manager).
  • Location - your device's GPS position is read once, at the moment you check in or out of a shift, to confirm you were at the venue. It is not tracked continuously or collected at any other time.
  • Attendance and schedule - clock-in/clock-out times, rota shifts, and shift-swap requests.
  • Pay information - hourly rates and calculated pay, visible to you and to managers at your restaurant.
  • Photos you upload - fridge/freezer photos, food safety checklist records, and supplier invoice photos, used only for the compliance and stock features you choose to use.
  • Profile picture - optional, set by you.

How it's used

Data is used only to operate the app's own features for your employer: recording attendance, calculating pay, running food safety checks, and tracking stock and invoices. We do not sell personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Invoice photos and AI

When a manager or staff member photographs a supplier invoice, the photo is sent to a third-party AI service (OpenAI) to read the items, quantities and prices off it automatically. That reading is always a draft - a person reviews and corrects it before anything is saved to stock records.

Who we share data with

We use a small number of service providers to run the app. None of them use your data for their own purposes - they process it only to provide their service to us:

  • Render - hosts the backend application.
  • Neon - hosts the database.
  • Amazon Web Services (S3) - stores uploaded photos.
  • OpenAI - reads invoice photos, as described above.

How long we keep it

Data is kept for as long as your account is active with your employer's restaurant. If a manager deactivates your account, your historical attendance and payroll records are retained for the business's own record-keeping (standard practice for employment records), but your account can no longer sign in.

Your rights

You can ask your manager (who controls the account) or contact us directly to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data, subject to the business's own legal obligations to retain employment records.

Children

Invisiko is a workplace tool and is not intended for use by children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will be updated.
